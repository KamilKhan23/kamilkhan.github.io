About Me

I build and study interactive 3D systems and the AI behind them, with a focus on extended reality. This summer at the National University of Singapore I worked with Dr. Anand Bhojan on 3D Gaussian Splatting for standalone VR, and built a system that lets people physically interact with Gaussian Splatting objects directly on a Meta Quest 3. That project is what convinced me I want to do research. Alongside graphics, I am interested in machine learning and reinforcement learning, especially for perception and intelligent agents inside 3D environments.

Before that I spent several years building real-time interactive software: mobile games in Unity at two studios, an on-device AR recognition app for my final year project, and a computer vision project on cross-view road-image correspondence. I want to keep working on immersive environments that are not only visually convincing but physically meaningful to interact with.

Having grown up across the United States, Canada, and Pakistan, I am interested in how interactive systems can be made accessible and engaging for people in very different places.

Research

Physics-Aware Gaussian Splatting Interaction in Standalone VR

NUS School of Computing, Summer Research Internship 2026 • Supervisor: Dr. Anand Bhojan • Jul – Sep 2026

  • Built a standalone VR system for physics-interactive manipulation of 3D Gaussian Splatting objects that runs natively on Meta Quest 3, without a tethered PC.
  • Designed and implemented a point-cloud-based convex-hull collision generation algorithm, 50–150x faster than standard voxelization-based approaches.
  • Designed a counterbalanced human-subjects study (IRB submitted) on physical interaction fidelity, using NASA-TLX and presence questionnaires.
  • Selected as 1 of 23 participants worldwide for the NUS Summer Research Internship.

Demo video available after paper submission.

Cross-View Correspondence for Road-Image Homography

Remote, with Dr. Fawad Ahmad, Rochester Institute of Technology • Jan – Feb 2026

Investigated automatically finding four corresponding anchor points between side-view and top-down road images to estimate a homography. Experimented with feature matching (ORB, SIFT/FLANN, LoFTR with RANSAC), geometric cues (Hough and LSD line intersections), and SegFormer road segmentation.

Publications

ARDetect: Offline AR Learning App for Electronics

Final Year Project (4-person team). An Android app that recognizes electronic components (breadboard, LED, Arduino) through the phone camera and places matching 3D models in AR, with an offline chatbot that answers questions about each part.

I owned the model side: merged datasets and trained a 3-class YOLOv8n detector (mAP50 of 0.993 / 0.848 / 0.995 for breadboard / LED / Arduino), then built the deployment pipeline that runs the model on-device as ONNX through Unity Sentis, fully offline.

Tech: Unity AR Foundation • YOLOv8n • ONNX • Unity Sentis • Python • Google Colab
